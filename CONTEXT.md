# Matrix Fellows

A research society supporting members' projects, opportunities and shared connections.

## Language

**Member dashboard**:
The personal space where a signed-in member maintains their profile, drafts,
mailbox connections, saved opportunities and next steps.
_Avoid_: Officer workspace

**Discovery workspace**:
The restricted research and review space for developing society connections with
labs, professors and research programs.

**Society steward**:
An officer or administrator authorized to maintain shared society information and
manage discovery. Ordinary membership does not grant stewardship.
