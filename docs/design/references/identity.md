# Matrix Fellows identity references

Research accessed 2026-09-07. This is art-direction research for an original SVG, not a license to reproduce the referenced identities.

## Primary sources

- [NASA: The Worm is Back!](https://www.nasa.gov/general/the-worm-is-back/) — NASA describes why its elaborate insignia was difficult to reproduce and why the cleaner Danne & Blackburn logotype, introduced in 1975, offered a simpler alternative. Useful principle: establish a recognizable silhouette with economical geometry, rather than adding several literal science symbols. Do not borrow NASA letterforms or its insignia.
- [Pentagram: MIT Media Lab](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab/story) — Michael Bierut's team built a fixed ML monogram on the prior identity's seven-by-seven grid, then used that underlying system for 23 research-group glyphs. Useful principle: let a disciplined geometric letterform establish identity; a research organization need not depend on a generic atom icon. Do not copy the ML monogram, its exact grid construction, or its subsidiary glyphs.
- User-supplied Matrix reference — a heavy M, diagonal orbital swoosh, and two satellite/star details. Preserve the idea of an M connected to discovery, but reduce crowded intersections and the dominant swoosh so the mark works in a compact header.

## Synthesis for the original mark

Use one clearly legible M and one open diagonal orbit, with generous negative space. Establish a consistent relationship between letter stroke and lighter orbital stroke; avoid three crossed ellipses, which recall the React/atom icon the user rejected. A single endpoint/node can suggest a researcher or discovery without requiring the reference's large star, multiple circles, or internal M highlight.

Judge the mark at actual header and mobile sizes, not only enlarged. Preserve monochrome recognition and use the site's accent only as an enhancement. Keep orbit/letter crossings visually separated and avoid thin crowded details that disappear at small sizes.

Hover is a short act of tracing, not a rotation: keep the letter silhouette stable while a highlight draws along existing paths with stroke-dashoffset, followed by a restrained endpoint emphasis. A roughly one-second eased sequence suits the site's deliberate motion. Reverse or fade smoothly on exit without resetting abruptly on repeated entry. Keyboard focus should receive the same treatment; reduced motion should retain the complete static mark with no tracing. These motion recommendations are project-specific synthesis, not claims about animations used by NASA or Pentagram.
