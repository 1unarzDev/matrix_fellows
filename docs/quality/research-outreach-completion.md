## Delivered architecture

The dashboard is for adults; existing live officer/admin appointment controls every draft and AI-outreach operation. Members retain profiles, research links, corrections and planning. No age-verification subsystem or ordinary-member promotion path was added. Historical school affiliation and actual opportunity eligibility remain factual.

The profile slice establishes one canonical authenticated self-profile without guessing legacy represented subjects. Join prefill imports only narrowly mapped self-reported fields. Artifacts save immutable source snapshots and shared project identities; candidate project facts remain separate from member contribution attribution, adoption and sharing. Local extraction retains exact source spans. Corrections and withdrawals persist; adopted eligible claims produce immutable projections. The overview/research-link flow is primary, with advanced manual fields optional.

The live outreach Workflow now uses the authoritative curated/source/Gemini adapters, dependent fenced stages, completed-evidence reuse and conservative atomic reservations. Coverage/freshness/project needs and available review capacity guide discovery. Added DFW sources and bounded GitHub, DOI, institutional HTML/PDF and GitHub Pages acquisition extend coverage without claiming comprehensive discovery or executing supplied code. Source review supports pagination, by-ID detail and hash-bound excerpts. Quota waits retain evidence and honor bounded provider reset metadata. Defaults remain 45 active targets, 15 weekly admissions, five per batch, two leases and three attempts; the queue cap is 60. Daily reservations default to 60 source requests, 30,720,000 source bytes, 15 model requests and 90,000 model tokens; each run permits twelve stages. Interrupted external submissions retain conservative charges rather than receiving an optimistic refund. Search and paid spend are not activated.

Persistent matches combine member/project/target evidence and practical preferences. Officer-selected match/project/angle reaches the writer, including supported stored model options. Unpaid Gemini receives reviewed nonpersonal technical excerpts only; identity, self-reported contribution, availability, signature and private voice preferences stay local. Private draft revisions use local previews with revision/editor-buffer conflict protection. Proposed emails retain sentence supports and exact consumed context. Relevant withdrawals, disabled subjects and factual corrections prevent future approval/send; unrelated background evidence does not rewrite the buffer or invalidate every proposal. The compatibility enum `student` remains stored while the product displays Personal inquiry.

Drafts remain private to the authorized current owner. Original authorship, represented subject, relationship owner, mailbox owner and approver are separate. Handoff clears approval/mailbox context, and the next owner approves from their own mailbox. Durable relationships preserve aliases, manual replies/outcomes, promises and canonical planner tasks. Owned approved follow-ups work while competing cold approaches and suppressed aliases remain blocked transactionally. Demotion retains only narrow historical mailbox disconnect and uncertain-outcome reconciliation. Immutable outbox snapshots, fingerprinting, fenced claims and uncertain-submission protection remain intact. Automatic inbox reading reports disconnected.
