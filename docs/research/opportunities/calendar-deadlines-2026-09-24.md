# Club calendar deadlines and event dates

Observed against official organizer sources on **2026-09-24**. This note is an
implementation handoff for the Matrix Fellows calendar, not a recurring date
template. A prior-year date must never be advanced by one year. `Confirmed`
means the organizer explicitly attached the date to the named cycle;
`tentative` means the organizer used tentative/TBD language or the page has an
internal conflict; `unannounced` means no calendar event should be created yet.

Deadline records and presentation/program records should remain distinct even
when they belong to one opportunity. Date-only deadlines must not be rendered
as midnight deadlines. Unless an official source states a time, store no time
or timezone.

### Davidson Fellows 2027

**Unannounced: do not create an exact deadline event.** The organizer says only
that “The 2027 application will open in the Fall of 2026.” The application is
for a significant completed piece of work in an eligible category. Applicants
must be age 18 or younger at the deadline and meet the published U.S.
citizenship/residency rules; teams may contain two eligible people. Every
application includes the category-specific materials, two nominator forms,
attachments, and applicant/parent commitments to attend the Washington, D.C.
reception if selected.

Official source and future submission route: [Davidson Fellows](https://www.davidsongifted.org/gifted-programs/fellows-scholarship/) and [eligibility/application requirements](https://www.davidsongifted.org/gifted-programs/fellows-scholarship/eligibility/). The February 18, 2026 date in older FAQ material is historical and must not be reused for 2027. Observed 2026-09-24.

### Texas JSHS 2026–27

**Unannounced: do not create a date event.** The official Texas region page says
“Symposium dates: TBD” and “Deadline for student applications: TBD.” Eligible
students are in grades 9–12 at public, private, or home schools in most of
Texas. Schools in the Trans-Pecos region or west of the published I-27/US-87/
I-20 boundary use Southwest JSHS; ask the regional director when a school is
near the boundary. The eventual submission is original STEM research through
the assigned regional symposium, not a direct national application.

Official source/future submission route: [Texas JSHS](https://www.jshs.org/region/texas/) and [regional competition rules](https://www.jshs.org/regional-competitions/core-rules-of-competition/). Regional registration is free. Observed 2026-09-24.

### HOSA Medical Innovation / Biomedical Innovation 2026–27

The official event name is **Medical Innovation**; “Biomedical Innovation” is a
useful search alias. Secondary teams contain 2–4 eligible HOSA competitors and
must advance through their chartered-association process. The deliverable is an
original medical innovation/prototype supported by evidence, plus one PDF
digital exhibit outline (no more than 13 content slides plus references). At
ILC, qualified teams bring the physical exhibit; teams surviving pre-judging
give a five-minute presentation.

## Calendar/admin and monitoring implications

- Store a record's `role` separately: `deadline`, `event/presentation`, `opens`,
  or `result`. A date can never be both the submission deadline and the event.
- Store source status separately from date role: `confirmed`, `tentative`, or
  `unannounced`. Unannounced records remain selectable/monitored but produce no
  calendar dot.
- Keep source evidence, official URL, submission URL, observed-at date, and
  original timezone wording on every dated record. Date-only values stay
  date-only.
- FWRSEF/TXSEF/ISEF and Texas JSHS/national JSHS are qualification chains, not
  duplicate open applications. HOSA likewise requires the local/Texas pathway.
- Monitor current annual pages at least daily during their expected
  announcement windows. Publish a changed date only after the existing
  two-observation/six-hour confirmation gate described in
  `docs/operations/opportunity-monitoring.md`; retain the last confirmed value
  and show a source-health warning on conflicts.
- Page conflicts requiring human review: Conrad homepage versus dedicated
  stage pages; SIMR's future date written in past tense; CMU AI Scholars' mixed
  2026/2027 block; BWSI's yearless March/April milestones; BU RISE's yearless
  December 17 opening; and FWRSEF/TXSEF territory disagreement for Eastland
  County. None affects Martin High School's Tarrant County route.
