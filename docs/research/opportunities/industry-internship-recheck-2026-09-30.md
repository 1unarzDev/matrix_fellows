## Amazon Future Engineer

Canonical organizer page: <https://www.amazonfutureengineer.com/scholarships>
(HTTP 200). Amazon directly links the administrator page:
<https://scholarshipamerica.org/scholarship/amazonfutureengineer/> (HTTP 200).
Use the unhyphenated administrator slug; the hyphenated variant returns 404.

**Classification:** scholarship with subsequent paid college software-engineering
internship opportunity. Primary computing/software; secondary electrical
engineering, data science, math and related computing fields. Texas eligible
within the national audience, subject to financial need, senior status and
work authorization. Do not represent the internship as immediate high-school
employment or promise a research paper.

Amazon quotes:

- “Applications for 2025–2026 are closed.”
- “Be a high school senior in the U.S.”
- “Must be authorized to work in the U.S.”
- “Must demonstrate financial need.”
- “Have a minimum cumulative grade point average of 2.3 on a 4.0 scale”.
- Students without a CS course “can opt-in to take an assessment offered by Amazon.”
- “up to $10,000 per year for four years and a paid summer internship at Amazon.”
- Internship teams “build projects that have a real impact on Amazon customers”;
  “At the end of the summer, most interns ship production-level code to Amazon!”
- Student testimonials explicitly describe internship “right after my first year
  of college” and “after just my first year of college”. These support college
  timing, not a universal guaranteed start date for future recipients.

Administrator adds: “Employment authorization must be valid for at least 2 years.”
Accepted majors include computer science/engineering, software engineering,
programming, mathematics/applied math, data science, electrical engineering,
transportation technology, computer information systems/science, cognitive
science, informatics and information technologies. Accredited four-year U.S.
college or accredited two-year college with intent to transfer.

**Materials:** teacher online recommendation (CS teacher preferred), transcript
showing student/school/grades/credit hours/terms, first two pages of household's
most recently filed IRS 1040. Official instruction: “Please black out any
sensitive data including Social Security Numbers before uploading.” Short
answers, activities/projects, work experience and household financial information
are considered. Optional course-substitute assessment has programming basics,
programming application, preferences/background, optional feedback; 45 minutes
total, individually timed; cannot retake within current cycle.

**Stages:** electronic application and documents → optional qualifying assessment
if needed → holistic Scholarship America selection → recipient supplies financial
aid award letter for last-dollar award calculation → renewal contingent on good
academic standing and continued eligibility → later paid industry internship
opportunity. Scholarship America, not Amazon employees, selects recipients.

**Funding:** up to $40,000 total, up to $10,000 annually covering unmet financial
need; actual award may be smaller. Renewable up to three more years. Internship
pay amount, duration, city, hours, housing, meals, travel, fees, age minimum and
independent-research policy are not stated on these pages: record unknown, not
free or remote. Applying online does not establish remote internship mode.

**Date conflict:** administrator says all documents by “January 26, 2026 at 3 PM
CT”, but application FAQ says “January 22, 2026 at 3 PM CT”. Both are historical;
do not silently select one, add a year, or put either on the upcoming calendar.
“Information for the upcoming cycle is tentative and subject to change.” Current
lifecycle closed / awaiting next announcement. Monitor both organizer and linked
administrator for new cycle, financial/work eligibility changes, materials,
scholarship deadlines, and later internship details. No verified future opening.

## Microsoft

Sources (HTTP 200):
<https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/discoveryprogram> and
<https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/students>.

Discovery is real paid high-school-graduate project work: four consecutive
full-time onsite weeks, mentors, small-team project on a single product team,
professional development and design/engineering/PM/UX exposure. Historically
2026 Atlanta July 6–31; Redmond July 13–August 7. No dated 2027 cycle verified.
“Applications open in early February” and interviews in March are yearless.

Eligibility exact quote: “High school graduating seniors that live and attend
high school within 50 miles of Redmond, Washington OR live and attend high school
around Atlanta, Georgia.” Redmond also requires membership/participation in
Microsoft-supported organizations. Atlanta specified school districts apply.
The main criteria and FAQ lists differ (FAQ additionally includes Clayton and
Douglas counties); this does not make Texas eligible.

Critical FAQ: “Your application will not be considered” if merely staying with
a friend/relative; must be enrolled in Western Washington school and live within
50 miles at application. Thus temporary relocation from Texas is not a workaround.
Graduating seniors must be rising bachelor's-degree freshmen. Math requirement
pre-calculus/equivalent or FAQ alternative three consecutive years including
senior year. No prior technical experience required. Resume must include applied/
accepted colleges, intended major and extracurricular/academic-support affiliations.
Resume submission → rolling review → interview → placement. Pay rate, citizenship,
age, housing, travel, meals and fees not established.

Other official current programs are not interchangeable: university internships
“open to current students in Bachelor’s, Master’s, MBA, PhD programs”; Explore
“specifically designed for first- and/or second-year college students.” No distinct
current Texas-accessible Microsoft high-school technical internship verified in
this audit. The `/highschool` URL redirects to an error page despite HTTP 200.
Discovery page's automated job feed currently includes unrelated senior/legal
jobs; never ingest those as Discovery placements by page association.

<https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/careers/candidates/students-early-careers/high-school.html>.

because Lockheed has Texas facilities. Individual future Texas roles need their

weekly hours and selection phases not specified here; don't invent them.

Old `lockheedmartinjobs.com/high-school` and search URLs redirect to corporate404;
Eightfold search returns a JS/config shell, not a verified specific vacancy.
