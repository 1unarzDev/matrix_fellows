# Texas summer-program eligibility audit

Reviewed against first-party organizer pages on **2026-09-23**. The catalog is
intended for students attending high school in Texas. A route remains eligible
when Texas students may apply nationally, internationally, remotely, or through
a Texas-specific pathway. A program restricted to another state, metro area, or
commuting radius is suppressed even if a Texas student could hypothetically
move there for the summer.

This is an eligibility audit, not an acceptance forecast. Residential cost,
citizenship, grade, age, nomination, and attendance requirements still apply.
“Texas eligible” also does not mean that the current application window is open.

## Added, verified routes

### MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute (BWSI)

- **Texas access:** the official FAQ requires students to reside in and
  physically attend high school in the United States and says participants are
  accepted from across the country.
- **Who:** grade 10 or 11 at application; current seniors and students below
  grade 10 are not eligible.
- **Preparation:** students first complete the relevant free online prerequisite,
  then submit the summer application and an adult recommendation.
- **Mode:** several AI/computing courses are virtual; in-person MIT courses do
  not provide housing.
- **2027 cost:** free below $200,000 family income with typical assets; $3,000
  above that threshold. Housing is excluded.
- **Sources:** [application](https://bwsi.mit.edu/apply-now/),
  [FAQ](https://bwsi.mit.edu/faq/), and
  [course list](https://bwsi.mit.edu/bwsi-programs/).

### MITES Summer

- **Texas access:** MITES describes Summer as a national program.
- **Who:** U.S. citizen or permanent-resident high-school juniors.
- **Format:** six-week residential advanced STEM program with applied electives,
  advising, and a final symposium.
- **Cost:** MITES states that the program is free and program-related costs are
  covered. The inspected page does not define current travel coverage.
- **Lifecycle:** the recurring program and eligibility are verified; exact next
  cycle dates were not published on the inspected page.
- **Source:** [MITES Summer](https://mites.mit.edu/discover-mites/mites-summer/).

### Anson L. Clark Scholars Program

- **Texas access:** hosted at Texas Tech in Lubbock and open nationally to U.S.
  citizens/permanent residents; no Texas-residency restriction is stated.
- **Who:** rising seniors and recent graduates, at least age 17 by the start.
- **Format:** seven weeks of faculty-mentored research for twelve scholars.
- **Cost/outcome:** reviewed 2026 rules list a $25 application fee, no
  participation fee, provided room/board/meals, excluded travel/personal/medical
  costs, and a $750 stipend after a successful research report.
- **Lifecycle:** 2026 is closed; no 2027 announcement was verified.
- **Sources:** [program](https://www.depts.ttu.edu/clarkscholars/),
  [benefits and eligibility](https://www.depts.ttu.edu/clarkscholars/ProgramDetails.php),
  and [application](https://www.depts.ttu.edu/clarkscholars/ApplicationDetails.php).

### Carnegie Mellon AI Scholars

- **Texas access:** the organizer calls it a national program drawing students
  from across the country.
- **Who:** grade 11, age 16+ by June 26, 2027, and U.S. citizen/permanent resident.
- **Format:** virtual Python preparation, then four residential weeks of AI
  coursework, faculty research exposure, and a collaborative project.
- **Cost:** fully funded tuition, housing, meals, and selected activities;
  limited travel assistance is available.
- **Evidence caution:** the page identifies a June 26, 2027 opening day while a
  nearby program-length line still says 2026. The catalog stores only the
  corroborated 2027 opening day and surfaces the conflict.
- **Source:** [CMU AI Scholars](https://www.cmu.edu/pre-college/academic-programs/ai_scholars.html).

### UC Davis Young Scholars Program

- **Texas access:** the rule is permanent U.S. residency plus current U.S.
  residence/school attendance, not California residency.
- **Who:** rising junior or senior, age 16 by the first day and not 18 before the
  last day.
- **Format:** six residential weeks of original biological, agricultural,
  environmental, or natural-science research.
- **Reviewed 2026 cost:** $45 application fee with waiver; $7,750 including
  housing, meals, activities, and five units of credit; need-based reductions.
- **Lifecycle:** 2026 is closed; no 2027 dates were verified.
- **Sources:** [overview](https://education.ucdavis.edu/young-scholars-program),
  [application](https://education.ucdavis.edu/ysp-application), and
  [cost](https://education.ucdavis.edu/ysp-program-cost).

### University of Iowa Secondary Student Training Program (SSTP)

- **Texas access:** no state-residency restriction is stated and the page also
  provides rules for international applicants.
- **Who:** grades 10–11.
- **Format:** five-and-a-half residential weeks in a faculty research group,
  ending with a poster.
- **Cost:** $95 application fee with waiver; the current page displays a $7,500
  program fee and aid up to 95% after acceptance.
- **2027 dates:** applications December 15, 2026–February 16, 2027; program June
  16–July 23, 2027.
- **Source:** [Iowa SSTP](https://belinblank.education.uiowa.edu/students/sstp/).

## Candidates deliberately not added

- **Michigan State HSHSP:** the official page says there will be no summer 2026
  program and does not announce a 2027 return.
- **Jackson Laboratory Summer Student Program:** the current admissions page
  says high-school seniors are no longer accepted.
- **Welch Summer Scholars:** no current first-party program or application page
  could be verified on the Welch Foundation site. It should not be restored from
  old aggregators without a current organizer page.
- **Stanford AIMI:** the prospective program page was not publicly retrievable
  during this audit. It remains a research candidate, not a publishable record.
- **NIH/local medical-center internships:** many require residence within a
  host-campus radius. They should be added only when a Texas site or a genuinely
  national residential/remote route is verified.

## Data rule going forward

Every published `Summer program` or `Internship` should carry explicit
`restrictions.geography` evidence. A missing restriction is not proof of
nationwide eligibility. Another-state local routes may remain suppressed for
history, but cannot appear in browse, search, facets, or detail endpoints.
