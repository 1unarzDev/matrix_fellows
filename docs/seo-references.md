# Search visibility setup for matrixfellows.com

Reviewed 2026-09-07 against official Google and Schema.org documentation. These are setup recommendations, not a claim that the live site is indexed, verified, or ranking. No Search Console account or DNS settings were accessed.

## Owner setup: Google Search Console

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/), add a **Domain** property, and enter `matrixfellows.com` without a protocol or path. Domain properties cover protocol and subdomain variations. DNS verification is required for this property type. [Google verification guide](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080?hl=en)
2. Choose TXT verification and copy the exact generated `google-site-verification=...` value. At the domain's authoritative DNS provider, **add** a TXT record at the apex (`@` or a blank host field, according to that provider). Preserve existing TXT, mail, and website records. Return to Search Console and click Verify once the record propagates. Keep the verification record after success. Google notes propagation can take minutes or days. [Google DNS verification instructions](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080?hl=en#domain_name_verification)
3. After the production sitemap is deployed, use the property's Sitemaps report to submit `https://matrixfellows.com/sitemap.xml`. Check its processing status. A `Sitemap: https://matrixfellows.com/sitemap.xml` line in robots.txt also supports discovery, but does not mean the sitemap was submitted in the account. Submission is a hint, not an indexing guarantee. [Build and submit a sitemap](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap)
4. Inspect `https://matrixfellows.com/` with URL Inspection, run a live test, check rendered HTML/screenshot and crawl/indexing eligibility, then request indexing if appropriate. This requires an owner or full-user account. Crawling can take days to weeks; repeated requests do not accelerate it and inclusion is not guaranteed. [Request recrawling](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl)

**Requires the owner:** the Google account, its generated verification value, and access to the authoritative DNS provider. These cannot be inferred from public HTML. Do not share passwords or session cookies. If DNS access is unavailable, a URL-prefix property for `https://matrixfellows.com/` supports alternative methods such as an HTML verification meta tag; that requires the exact user-generated value and verifies a narrower scope. A meta tag does **not** verify a Domain property. [Verification methods](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080?hl=en)

## Implementation priorities

- **Keep meaningful content in the initial HTML.** Google can render JavaScript, but app-shell content depends on rendering. Google recommends server-side rendering or prerendering for speed and because not all bots execute JavaScript. Retain descriptive headings, society identity, research opportunities, contact/join information, and normal links in the HTML; progressively enhance visuals. This is a recommendation, not a claim that client-rendered sites cannot be indexed. [JavaScript SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics)
- **Do not make essential content depend on a click.** Google Search does not interact with pages to reveal lazy-loaded content. Ensure important content is available without opening navigation, dismissing a loader, or clicking a button. Test the rendered result; a browser view alone does not establish what Google indexed. [Lazy-loaded content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/lazy-loading)
- **Treat section anchors as one page.** `/#about` and `/#opportunities` can remain useful in-page navigation, but should not be presented as independent sitemap pages. Google says not to use fragments to load distinct page content. If separate search landing pages are needed later, give them real URLs with accessible content and crawlable `<a href>` links; that is a separate content/routing project. [History API instead of fragments](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics#use-history-api)
- **Keep the sitemap honest.** For a genuinely single-page site, list the absolute canonical homepage once. Include additional URLs only when they are real pages intended for search. Omit `lastmod` unless a reliable last-significant-update date exists; do not substitute every build's time. Google ignores `priority` and `changefreq`. [Sitemap rules](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap)
- **Use accurate organization markup.** Google recommends the most specific `Organization` subtype that actually matches the entity, on the homepage or an organization information page. `EducationalOrganization` means an educational organization; it is not required just because members are students. For an independent student research society without evidence of a more specific institutional identity, `Organization` is the conservative recommendation. Include factual name, description, canonical URL, crawlable logo, and only genuine organization profiles in `sameAs`. Do not invent legal status, affiliations, addresses, or dates. Google's logo guidance specifies at least 112 × 112 px and a crawlable/indexable image URL. Structured data can help understanding but does not guarantee a special search appearance. [Google Organization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization), [Schema.org definition](https://schema.org/EducationalOrganization)
- **Prioritize usable performance, not score chasing.** Google's targets are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP below 200 ms, and CLS below 0.1. For an animated site, prioritize readable initial content, responsive interactions, and stable layout. Core Web Vitals participate in ranking, but good scores do not guarantee top placement, and Google cautions against pursuing perfect scores solely for SEO. Relevant, useful content still matters. [Core Web Vitals](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals), [Page experience and ranking](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience)

## Completion boundaries

Deployable metadata, sitemap, robots rules, and structured data are technical foundations. They do not establish Search Console verification, successful sitemap processing, actual indexing, or any ranking position. Those must be checked after deployment in the owner's property. Evaluate search performance over time rather than promising an immediate first-place result. [Sitemap limitations](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap), [Recrawling limitations](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl), [Ranking limitations](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience)
