# Workshops and publication routes — checked 2026-09-07

Primary-source review produced five publication cards, five workshop/learning
cards, and one conference card. Catalog files carry the sources, eligibility
caveats, costs, and dated evidence used for publication. No acceptance guarantee
or invented prestige ranking is implied. Agent research was unavailable due to
usage limits; the primary agent fetched the official pages directly.

## Publications

- [JEI submission guide](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/guidelines),
  [author eligibility](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/author-eligibility),
  [fees](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/pay-and-submit/pay), and
  [review process](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/review-process):
  age13+, middle/high school, adult submission and senior coauthor; $49 fee with
  limited scholarships. No expedited review. Do not describe publication as a
  fast-track service or infer year-round acceptance from the submission button.
- [NHSJS](https://nhsjs.com/about/) and its
  [submission guide](https://nhsjs.com/submission-guidelines/): high-school
  original research and science/policy writing; stated scientific peer review.
  “Free online journal” was not interpreted as proof of zero author fees.
- [Young Scientists Journal publishing](https://www.youngscientistsjournal.com/publish)
  and [about](https://www.youngscientistsjournal.com/about): ages12–20, original
  research/reviews/science writing; student editors and academic advisors.
  Publication may take months. Do not equate blog posts with research articles.
- [JOSS requirements](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html) and
  [about](https://joss.theoj.org/about): free professional peer-reviewed software
  journal; requires significant research use, public history over six months,
  documentation, tests, and open development. No explicit school-year exclusion
  found, but this is not a high-school program or guaranteed fit for CRANE.
- [arXiv submission guide](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html): free,
  registered author, possible endorsement, moderation, distribution license.
  Preprint dissemination is not journal peer review. Its daily processing cutoff
  is not an annual submission deadline.

No inspected publication page provided sufficiently explicit year-round wording
to label it rolling. The model/schema now support that status only with exact
positive evidence; lack of a deadline alone is insufficient.

## Workshops and related research venues

- [ML4PS2026](https://ml4physicalsciences.github.io/2026/): September12 deadline
  explicitly includes2026 and23:59 AoE; December11 event. **Independent of NeurIPS
  in2026**, Georgia Tech/Atlanta; virtual participation possible. Four-page
  research, evaluations/datasets, or perspective tracks. October10 notification
  lacks an explicit year in its line and was not auto-converted into a checkpoint.
- [CCAI NeurIPS2026](https://www.climatechange.ai/events/neurips2026): non-archival
  papers/proposals/tutorial notebooks. August29 deadline passed, September29
  decisions upcoming. December11/12 is ambiguous, so no single event day invented.
  Their AI-use restriction during paper writing is relevant to authors; this
  catalog is not a submission-writing service.
- [CoRL workshop call](https://www.corl.org/contributions/call-for-workshops):
  November12,2026, Austin. Individual contributed-paper dates differ from the
  workshop-organizer proposal deadline; the latter is deliberately not listed as
  a student paper opportunity. Tutorials need not require papers.
- [SciPy2026](https://www.scipy2026.scipy.org/): July13–19 completed, tutorials
  July13–14 and sprints July18–19. Educational route, not a research award. Future
  edition not announced in inspected page. Keep last edition history.
- [CCAI virtual school](https://www.climatechange.ai/events/summer_school2026):
  registration closed, recorded lectures and asynchronous coding notebooks;
  September20 certificate completion applies to **existing participants only**.
  US$30/10 geographic fee tiers and waivers. The detailed page's July20 start
  takes precedence over the event index's conflicting July19 entry.
- [ML4H](https://ml4h.ahli.cc/) and
  [CFP](https://ml4h.ahli.cc/submit/call-for-papers/): now a standalone symposium,
  correctly categorized Conference rather than padding Workshop counts. September10
  deadline; December6–7 event. Eight-page archival Proceedings vs four-page
  non-archival Findings. Ethics approval and anonymization are material requirements.

Advanced venues do not explicitly promise high-school eligibility in these
calls; cards disclose that and recommend organizer confirmation, especially for
minors attending in person. No school-year restriction found is not a guarantee.

## Exclusions and monitoring

Curieux's [old submission page](https://www.curieuxacademicjournal.com/submissions)
says it no longer publishes new papers and redirects authors to The Curieux
Review. It was not added as an active journal without verifying the successor.
JSR fetches failed or returned an empty page, so it was not published from memory.
ML4PS's root uses a meta-refresh; the verified2026 destination is used directly.

All new cards use the existing evidence-gated annual monitors; old dates are not
advanced by adding a year. Blocked or ambiguous checks retain the researched
record with a freshness warning. Curated costs/descriptions still require editorial
review; automated dates/lifecycle checks cannot guarantee all guidance stays current.
